My Top 5 Videogames

This is the most important list ever made

  1. Final Fantasy XIV

    2. Final Fantasy XIV is a massively online role-playing game created by Square Enix. Created as a love letter to the franchise itself, this game is full of charm and wonderful story moments, not to mention incredibly fun gameplay

  2. Kingdom Hearts 2

    3. Kingdom Hearts 2 is an action role-playing game developed by Square Enix. Blending together the worlds of Disney and Final Fantasy, it created countless unforgetable moments in my childhood

  3. Marvel Rivals
    4. >

    >

    Marvel Rivals is a "hero shooter" developed by NetEase, and currently features over 50 heroes you can play as in intense battles, it's very fun and my favorite heroes to play with are Cyclops, Jeff the landshark, and Emma Frost

    >

  4. Baldurs Gate 3

    5. Baldurs Gate 3 is a role-playing game developed by Larian Studios. It takes place in the Dungeons and Dragons world, and allows you to experience the fun of DnD in the comfort of your own home. The story is a treat as well, and the gameplay perfectly reflects the tabletop game

  5. Death Stranding(1 and 2)

    6. That's right, I'm cheating and putting both games in the same spot. Death Stranding is an action-adventure game developed by Kojima Productions. The game takes place in a world just like our own that suffered an apocalyptic event known as "the Death Stranding", leaving humanity living disconnected from one another and in fear of the world outside. You play as Sam "Porter" Bridges, a delivery man who is tasked with reconnecting America and the plot gradually expands in scope from there. It's the most surreal experience I've ever had with a game and yet, it's presented so sincerely that I was utterly captivated with the world and its characters. The Sequel, Death Stranding 2, is much the same and I couldn't put one without the other.
