My Collection

This is my roughly my current top 6 favorite games, for Final Fantasy 14 specifically it's my favorite video game of all time, while the rest will rotate out on occassion. I think video games are just so special, they've taken me to fantastical worlds and experiences with stories both told through the game and ones I've created by playing myself that I'll remember for years and years. They have helped me get through some of the toughest times of my life, because we all need a place to escape to when reality gets too much. Video games are a thing that I have zero doubt will be a core piece of my daily life and identity until my last days.

Final Fantasy XIV

MMORPG | 2013 | Square Enix

I've said it once and I'll say it again, Final Fantasy XIV is my favorite video game of all time. The game itself has its foundations as a love letter to the other previous 13 installments, and the 2 sequels. But it has grown into something beyond that, with a world, story, and cast of characters i could spend hours gushing over. There's also so many things to do inside the game, from fishing to monster catching to crafting to having amazing battles. I've been playing for 6 and a half years and there's still things I haven't done or accomplished, and I'll happily play for another 6 and beyond.

Marvel Rivals

Game | 2024 | Hero Shooter | NetEase

This game will very likely also stay on my favorite games of all time list, for 2 simple facts: It's very fun and it stars Marvel heroes. I have a deep love for Marvel comics that the developers for this game share, shown through the work in the character designs and animations, and the stories they tell in the game itself. Aside from that playing as my favorite Marvel heroes is a dream come true, and swinging around as Spider-Man or beating people up as The Hulk or Rogue is the epitome of fun! I'll never get tired of playing this game

Hades 2

Game | 2025 | Rogue-like | Supergiant Games

Hades 2 is a game based in Greek Mythology, and the story centers around an original character named Melinoe, the daughter of Hades. Chronos somehow returned and took over the underworld, and it's our job to save it! The game operates on individual attempts where you try to save Hades in one-shot, gathering up a bunch of powers from the Greek Pantheon(your uncles, aunts, and cousins) along the way. I really enjoy this style of game, it has a very addictive nature of gaining a random assortment of powers that are always different in each attempt. And there a lot of really special parts going on in the game, the obvious being that the Greek gods are all portrayed fairly accurately with beautiful illustrations, and you have frequent conversations with them as they help you on your quest. The other special part is that there is an insane amount of dialogue recorded for your performance, if you keep losing to one thing, your character will have conversations about self doubt, and other characters will give advice and encouragement. It always feel like you're discovering something new no matter what you're doing.

Fire Emblem Engage

Game | 2023 | Strategy | Intellegent Systems

This game is quite simply super fun, the story is a bit on the lighter side and plays out like a saturday morning cartoon. But that doesn't detract from the fun whatsoever. The combat is super flashy, the music is fun and intense, and all the characters are varying degrees of insane, with each person thinking "Man, all of these other guys are crazy". Similiar to Final Fantasy 14, this game celebrates the franchise and all the games before it, they do this by having a special ring that stores the spirits of famous characters from past games. And when the Main Character says "Engage!", the spirit powers them up. It's very whimsical and fun, and it's something of a comfort game for me.

Xenoblade Chronicles 2

Game | 2017 | RPG | Monolith Soft

Xenoblade Chronicles 2 is a very long, deep JRPG that's similiar to Fire Emblem Engage in how cartoony it gets. But the charm lies in how sincere the game delivers those silly moments. The game takes place in a world that's largely water, with gigantic turtle creatures serving as its continents, and they're slowly dying out, and our hero tries to find a way to find a new home for humanity. The game is extremely anime-coded, for better and some might say for worse, but not me. There's a lot of characters to meet and play as, and the world and music are beautiful. The story can be broken down into "The Undying Optimism of a Boy fights the Nihilism of everything". The main character remains rather static in terms of development but his undying optimism in the face of seemingly hopeless world changes the lives of everyone he comes across, and it's a pretty amazing journey and experience.

Monster Hunter Rise

Game | 2021 | Monster Hunter| Capcom

I have the least to say about this game, but it is by no means lesser then the other games I've shared. The premise of the game is in the title: you hunt monsters. What makes the game special is how you do so, in the most bombastic way possible. You have swords twize the size of your body, Axes that transform into swords, swords that transform into buzzsaws, giant guns, lances, lances that explode, and more. The monsters themselves are the size of buildings, and all of them are a blast to fight. The story is pretty light and just an excuse to fight more monsters, and I'm not complaining. The game is fun, addicting, and is full of unforgettable moments.
