About Me

My name is Mauricio Tapia, I was born on July 24th, 1996 in Dallas, Texas. My parents came to America from Mexico, and I am blessed to be surrounded by my heritage everyday. I own a cat, a dog, and a rowdy collection of tropical fish. Lastly I'm currently attending Texas Tech and learning web design, it's a very rewarding process and I hope to launch into an exciting and fulfilling future with this knowledge. Lastly, I grew up deeply in love with Penguins, to the point of it becoming a childhood nickname!

Hobbies & Interests

To get a better understanding of who I am, I've displayed the activities that make up who I am today

Gaming

Videogames have been associated with my earliest memories, and have served as a source of the forming and strengthening of bonds with friends and family. The worlds I've explored and experiences I've shared will forever stick with me.

Hiking

I fell in love with Hiking during my youth due to being in the Boyscouts of America, and when I get the chance I always try to make a trip to local trails to enjoy the breathtaking views and connect with nature.

Final Fantasy 14

While it may feel redundant to include seperately, Final Fantasy 14 is the shining pillar of everything Gaming has done for me growing up, and was an essential part of uplifting me and select family during some of our hardest times. Thus it'll always have a special spot in my heart.

Reading & Writing
